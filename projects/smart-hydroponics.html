Smart Hydroponics System

A dual-zone farming setup designed to improve plant growth consistency through automated monitoring and control. The system tracks key environmental variables and supports better decision-making for crop health.

ESP32 I2C & GPIO 3D Design

Objective

  • Automate nutrient and climate control
  • Improve crop consistency across zones
  • Integrate sensors with microcontroller logic

Key Components

  • pH sensor
  • EC monitoring
  • Temperature and humidity sensors
  • Custom 3D-printed housing

Outcome

  • Cleaner automation workflow
  • Better environmental visibility
  • Useful prototype for controlled growing environments
